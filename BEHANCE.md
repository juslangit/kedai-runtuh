## Description

**Kedai Runtuh** is a casual mobile game about stacking a mamak order — plates,
bowls, cups of teh, takeaway cartons — as high as you can before the whole thing
goes over.

Food hangs from a rope swinging above the table. One tap drops it. There is no
tutorial and no menu to learn: the entire game is a single thumb and a sense of
timing. Land a piece dead centre and you get a PERFECT worth double. When the
tower starts leaning it creaks and the camera trembles, so a collapse is something
you can feel coming rather than something that just happens to you.

**The design brief was a three-second test.** Could a stranger understand the whole
game from three seconds of muted video? That question drove every decision. The
camera is locked side-on so the tower's silhouette reads at a glance. The physics
is locked to a flat plane — the game is 3D to look at and 2D to play — so nothing
that decides the outcome happens where the player cannot see it. And when you
lose, time drops to a third, the camera shakes, the dishes go over in waves, and
it holds on the wreckage before showing your score. Losing is the part worth
watching.

**Built in Godot 4**, with a physically simulated rope, a pendulum swing, and every
piece of food fitted to its collision box rather than the other way round — so the
art can be swapped without ever retuning the game.

## Things worth calling out in the post

- The **collapse** is deliberately the loudest moment in the game, because failure
  is what people share.
- **Sixteen items** and a full kitchen, all fitted to a fixed physics footprint so
  any piece can balance on any other.
- The tower **creaks and trembles** before it falls — turning luck into tension.
- Runs from **grey boxes to a finished build** with menus, settings, sound, haptics
  and an Android APK.
